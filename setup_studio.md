# My Studio Setup

Hey! Sometimes I speak with other artists, and we often exchange secrets about what tools we use for what, or how we achieve a certain sound. So here is a comprehensive list of what I actually use in my setup as of 09/2026. Sadly, like most of us, I have collected way too many plugins and pieces of software over the years.

If you know of something that's better than what I currently have, or could replace two tools with one, please let me know. I'm always looking to refine my setup and make it as maximalist minimalist as possible.

I do believe there is creativity in limitation.
